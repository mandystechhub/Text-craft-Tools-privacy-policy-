Privacy Policy

TextCraft Tools — Text Formatting & Utility App

Effective date: 28 October 2026

TextCraft Tools ("the App") is a text utility application that helps users format, clean, and transform text on their Android devices.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Information Collection

TextCraft Tools is designed to provide its core functionality without requiring account registration or login.

The App does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information to the developer through its core offline text-processing features.

Text entered into the App may contain personal information. Users should avoid entering sensitive information unless necessary for their intended use.

2. Text Processing

TextCraft Tools processes text to provide its available formatting and transformation features.

The core text-processing functions are designed to work locally on your device without requiring an internet connection.

The App does not intentionally send the text you enter to the developer or an external server as part of these offline functions.

3. Storage and Clipboard

Depending on the features you use, the App may temporarily handle text in application memory or use your device's clipboard when you choose to copy text.

Copied text may remain in your device's clipboard and could be accessible to other applications according to your Android version and device settings.

You are responsible for reviewing text before copying or sharing it with another application.

4. Internet Connection and Data Sharing

The App's core functionality is designed for offline use.

The App does not intentionally transmit text entered by users to external servers through its core offline features.

The developer does not sell personal information collected through the App.

If future versions introduce online functionality or third-party services that change how information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated as appropriate.

5. Third-Party Services

The App is intended to operate without advertising or analytics services.

The developer does not intentionally use third-party services to collect personal information through the App's core functionality. Actual data practices depend on the libraries and software components included in the published build.

6. Data Security

TextCraft Tools is designed to process text locally. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should avoid entering passwords, financial information, or other confidential information unless they understand the risks associated with their intended use.

7. Children's Privacy

TextCraft Tools is a general-purpose utility application and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children through its core offline functionality.

8. Your Choices

You can choose what text to enter, process, copy, or share through the App.

You can clear text from the App using the available controls, where supported, and manage clipboard content through your device's available features.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated policy will be published on the page where this policy is hosted, along with a revised effective date when appropriate.
